---
title: Rivet adapter status
description: Cable's Rivet adapter is beta and has not passed its real hibernation conformance gate, so it remains private and unsuitable for stable application deployments.
---

`@cable/rivet` is a beta adapter. It remains incomplete until its real Rivet hibernation conformance matrix passes. Do not treat it as a stable runtime choice.

The package exports `rivetHost()` for a channel actor and `createHandler()` for an authenticated edge gateway. It is private and unpublished.
